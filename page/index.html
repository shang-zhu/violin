Violin translates educational videos with native-voice dubbing and aligned subtitles, so a lecture in English can be heard in Chinese, a history lesson in Mandarin can be watched in Spanish. Learning without borders, preserved in its own voice.

✦
I.

Native Voices

Language-matched voices — a Korean narrator for Korean, a French narrator for French — no flat machine tone.

II.

Aligned Subtitles

Every video ships with a timestamped SRT, so learners can read along, pause, and revisit any passage.

III.

Speaker Diarization

Multiple speakers in one lecture each receive a distinct, consistent voice throughout the translation.

IV.

42 Languages

From Mandarin to Swahili — a single pipeline spanning forty-two languages, powered by Cartesia Sonic 3.
